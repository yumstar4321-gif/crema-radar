Put product photos here, then add the filename to that product in data/products.yaml:

  image: "barista-express.jpg"

Only use photos you took yourself, or ones the brand has given you permission to use
(many brands share images through a press/media page if you email them).

Never download or screenshot images from Amazon: it's against Amazon's Associates rules.
Once the Amazon API is switched on (phase 2), Amazon's own product image appears
automatically and takes priority over these.

Tip: square or 4:3 photos on a plain light background look best. Keep each under ~300 KB.
