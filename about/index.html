About Crema Radar

Crema Radar tracks home coffee gear on Amazon Australia: espresso machines, grinders, pod machines and accessories. The goal is simple: tell you when something is genuinely cheaper than usual, and stay quiet when it isn't.

How we pick deals

  • Every product on our watchlist is checked each morning.
  • A product only counts as a deal when Amazon's own discount is meaningful. Discounts measured against a list price (RRP) need to be bigger before we flag them, because RRPs are often prices nobody pays.
  • We show the time each price was checked. Prices change, sometimes within minutes, so always confirm on Amazon.
  • We note when an item is sold by a third-party seller rather than Amazon AU.

How we make money

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. When you buy through a link on this site, Amazon pays us a small commission. It doesn't change the price you pay, and nobody pays us to include or rank a product.
